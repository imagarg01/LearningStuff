# Master Curriculum: Deep-Dive Equity Investing & Market Timing

> **Goal:** Master the complete 360-degree art and science of equity investing—evaluating business models and moats, forensic accounting (for both non-financials and Banks/NBFCs), management integrity, decoding media/PR spin vs. reality, valuation & market cycle timing, Private Equity/IPO exit mechanics, special situations, position sizing, and a repeatable idea-generation workflow.

---

## Complete 12-Module Curriculum Structure

### [Module 1: Decoding the Business Model, Unit Economics & Moat](./01-Business-Model-And-Moat.md)
1. **Unit Economics & Value Proposition:** Core unit of sale, contribution margin waterfall, CAC vs. LTV, and store/plant payback periods (<3 years).
2. **Revenue Quality & Predictability:** The 4 Tiers of Revenue (Recurring, Repeat, Transactional, and Lumpy EPC/Tender) and why the market assigns vastly different P/E multiples to each.
3. **The Value Chain & Bargaining Power:** Pricing power over customers ("Low Cost, High Consequence" B2B moats) vs. vulnerability to suppliers.
4. **Economic Moats (Competitive Advantage):** Switching costs, network effects, structural cost advantages, intangible assets, and spotting *moat erosion* vs. *moat widening*.
5. **Operating Leverage vs. Financial Leverage:** Fixed-cost vs. variable-cost structures and why profits explode (or collapse) faster than revenues.
6. **Industry Structure & The Capital Cycle:** Rational oligopolies, unorganized-to-organized shift, "Picks and Shovels", and tracking industry capacity cycles (why supply kills returns).

---

### [Module 2: Financial Statement Forensics (Balance Sheet, P&L, and Cash Flow)](./02-Financial-Statement-Forensics.md)
1. **The 3-Statement Inter-Linkage:** How P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow connect—and why profitable companies go bankrupt due to accrual accounting.
2. **Balance Sheet Deep-Dive:**
   - Asset Quality: Gross Block & Fixed Asset Turnover, CWIP (genuine expansion vs. "Zombie CWIP"), Goodwill/Intangibles (hollow net worth).
   - Working Capital & Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC): Inventory days, Receivable days (channel stuffing), Payable days.
   - Debt & Solvency: Debt-to-Equity, Interest Coverage, the "High Cash + High Debt" fraud paradox, and contingent liabilities.
3. **Cash Flow Truths:** Cumulative 5-year CFO to EBITDA conversion (>70%), Free Cash Flow (FCF), and Maintenance Capex vs. Growth Capex.
4. **Return Ratios & DuPont Analysis:** ROE, ROCE, and Core ROIC (ex-cash)—deconstructing whether returns come from Margin, Asset Turnover, or Leverage.
5. **Accounting Red Flags & Shenanigans:** Capitalizing operating expenses, channel stuffing, depreciation tweaks, bad-debt under-provisioning, and "Other Income" padding.

---

### [Module 3: Management Quality, Capital Allocation & Corporate Governance](./03-Management-And-Governance.md)
1. **Capital Allocation Track Record:** The 5 ways to deploy cash, reinvesting at high Incremental ROIC (ROIIC) vs. "diworsification" (the foreign acquisition curse), dividends, and buybacks.
2. **Skin in the Game & Alignment:** Promoter shareholding trends, share pledging perils, executive remuneration as % of PAT (<5–8%), and generational succession.
3. **Corporate Governance & Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** Detecting value leakage to private promoter entities, real estate rent siphoning, brand royalties, and competing unlisted group companies.
4. **The "Say vs. Do" Audit:** The 3-year time-machine test comparing past Annual Report promises against actual delivery.

---

### [Module 4: Reading Between the Lines (Media, Concalls & Narrative vs. Reality)](./04-Reading-Between-The-Lines.md)
1. **Dissecting Earnings Calls (Concalls) & MD&A:** The Corporate-Speak to Real-English translation dictionary, catching shifting goalposts in investor presentations, and CEO vs. CFO body language.
2. **Decoding Media Appearances & PR:** Why CEOs suddenly appear on TV every week (pre-QIP, promoter stake sale, or retail distribution at peak cycle), and separating structural tailwinds from temporary windfalls (Volume vs. Price/Realization growth split).
3. **Footnotes & Statutory Disclosures:** Mid-term Auditor/CFO resignations, reading free Credit Rating Rationale reports (CRISIL/ICRA/CARE), and cash tax rate sanity checks.
4. **Scuttlebutt & Cross-Verification:** Cross-checking competitor concalls, supplier commentary, and industry data.

---

### [Module 5: Valuation, Market Cycles & Solving the "Opportunity Cost" Problem](./05-Valuation-Cycles-And-Timing.md)
1. **The Twin Engines of Stock Returns:** $\text{Stock Return} = \text{Earnings Growth} \times \text{P/E Multiple Change}$. Why buying a 20% grower at 70x P/E yields 0% return for 3 years (Time Correction / De-rating).
2. **Expectations Investing:** Reverse-engineering what growth is already priced into the current stock price; trading on *Delta* (change in expectations).
3. **Business & Sector Cycles:** Structural compounders vs. Cyclicals; avoiding the deadly low-P/E trap at the top of a cyclical commodity peak.
4. **Catalysts & Inflection Points:** Entering when triggers are 1–2 quarters away (capex commissioning, operating leverage, hidden high-margin segment mix shift, balance sheet de-leveraging).
5. **Price-Volume & Institutional Clues:** Stan Weinstein's 4-Stage Market Cycle (Stage 1 Base, Stage 2 Markup Breakout above 200-DMA, Stage 3 Distribution Top, Stage 4 Markdown).
6. **Selling Rules:** The 4 valid reasons to sell a stock.

---

### [Module 6: The PE/VC Playbook, IPO Mechanics & Institutional Exit Games](./06-PE-VC-And-IPO-Mechanics.md)
1. **PE/VC Lifecycle & Incentives:** The 7–10 year fund life clock, LP/GP dynamics, forced exits, and pre-IPO valuation ratchets.
2. **Pre-IPO "Window Dressing" (Dressing Up the Bride):** Starving marketing/R&D to juice margins, channel stuffing, fake "Adjusted EBITDA" metrics, LBO debt hangovers, and **Disguised OFS via Related-Party Consolidation**.
3. **Reading the DRHP Like a Detective:** The **3-Lens Expert OFS Framework** (when a 100% OFS is natural for a 60%-ROCE cash cow vs. when it's a red flag), WACA, Peer Table tricks, and the **">1% Marquee Institutional Register" Audit** (Sovereign Wealth Funds & Endowments vs. Hedge Fund Flippers).
4. **Post-IPO Lock-In Expiries, Supply Overhangs & SEBI's 75% MPS Rule:** Day 30/90/180 lock-ins, the **IGIL Case Study** (76.55% Blackstone + 90.81% Institutional Lock-up + Dec 2027 MPS Free-Float & MSCI/Nifty Passive Index Catalyst).
5. **Secondary Market Games:** The "Brokerage Report + Media Blitz + Block Deal" routine, opportunistic QIPs vs. promoter preferential warrants.
6. **Profiting from PE Behavior:** Buying the "final tranche" forced PE exit and backing PE control-buyout turnarounds.

### [Module 7: Bridging Your Personal Gaps — From "Strategy Consultant" to "Forensic Investor" (IGIL Case Study)](./07-Bridging-Your-Gaps-Consultant-To-Investor.md)

2. **5 Gap-Filling Habits:**
   - The "Rule of Page 1 Valuation" (Reverse DCF before narrative).
   - Cross-Module Contradiction Hunting (testing Module 1 claims against Module 2 balance sheet reality).
   - The "Customer Health Ceiling" Test (why 60% margins can't expand when customers make 3%).
   - Tracing the Cash in IPOs & M&A ("Who actually gets the money?").
   - Checking the Shareholder Supply Calendar.
3. **The Mandatory 1-Page 360-Degree Gatekeeper Scorecard:** Copy-paste template required before approving any stock.

### [Module 8: Analyzing Banks, NBFCs & Financial Stocks (The BFSI Forensic Rulebook)](./08-BFSI-Banks-NBFCs-Forensics.md)
1. **Why Non-Financial Rules Fail in BFSI:** Why high Debt-to-Equity and negative CFO are normal in growing lenders.
2. **The ROA $\times$ Leverage DuPont Tree:** Deconstructing Yield, Cost of Funds (CASA Moat), NIM, Cost-to-Income, PPOP, Credit Cost, ROA (>2%), and ROE (>16%).
3. **Asset Quality Forensics (How Lenders Hide Bad Loans):** GNPA, NNPA, Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR >70%), Loan Evergreening, Denominator Management (why you must check Lagged Slippages), and SMA-1/SMA-2 buckets.
4. **The #1 Killer of NBFCs — ALM (Asset-Liability Mismatch):** Borrowing 3-month Commercial Paper to lend for 15 years.
5. **Valuing Lenders & Non-Lending BFSI Platforms:** The Price-to-Book (P/B) vs. ROA/ROE matrix, plus Exchanges, Depositories, AMCs, and Insurance metrics.

### [Module 9: Macro, Government Policy Traps & Special Situations (Demergers, Buybacks & Turnarounds)](./09-Macro-Policy-And-Special-Situations.md)

3. **Special Situations (Time-Bound Catalysts):**

   - **Buybacks:** Promoter-Excluded Tender Offer Buybacks (Bullish) vs. Open Market Buybacks (PR Stunt).
   - **Management Takeovers & Open Offers (SEBI SAST).**

### [Module 10: Portfolio Construction, Position Sizing, Pyramiding & Behavioral Psychology](./10-Portfolio-Sizing-Pyramiding-And-Psychology.md)

4. **Mastering Behavioral Biases:** Overcoming Anchoring Bias (to past prices or your buy price), Sunk Cost/Effort Bias, Confirmation Bias, and Boredom Selling in Stage 1.

### [Module 12: The Master Financial Ratios Encyclopedia & Forensic Reference](./12-Master-Financial-Ratios-Encyclopedia.md)
1. **Return & Capital Efficiency Ratios (The Moat Detectors):** ROE, ROCE (and how Screener's default formula gets distorted by IPO cash & M&A goodwill), Core ROIC, Incremental ROIC (ROIIC), and ROA.
2. **Profitability & Margin Ratios (The Pricing Power Detectors):** Gross Profit Margin %, EBITDA Margin (OPM %), and Net Profit Margin (PAT %) — separating pricing power from operating leverage and "Other Income" padding.
3. **Working Capital & Operational Efficiency Ratios (The Cash Trap Detectors):** Debtor Days, Inventory Days, Payable Days (Dominance vs. Distress), Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC), and Fixed Asset / Gross Block Turnover.
4. **Cash Flow & Earnings Quality Ratios (The Fraud Detectors):** CFO / EBITDA (>70% rule), Free Cash Flow (FCF), and Maintenance vs. Growth Capex.
5. **Solvency & Balance Sheet Survival Ratios (The Bankruptcy Detectors):** Debt-to-Equity (separating Ind-AS 116 Lease Liabilities from real bank debt), Net Debt-to-EBITDA, Interest Coverage Ratio, and why a *high* Current Ratio (>3x) is often a forensic red flag.
6. **Valuation Multiples (The Price Tag Detectors):** Trailing vs. Forward P/E, Forward PEG (vs. Screener's backward-looking PEG), EV / EBITDA, Price-to-Book (P/B), and FCF Yield %.
7. **Per-Share Metrics & The Master Ratio Pairing Cheat Sheet:** Basic vs. Diluted EPS, Dividend Payout Ratio, and the 12-row lookup table of **Mandatory Companion Ratios** so no ratio ever fools you in isolation.
8. **The "Crook Founder" Forensic Ratio Playbook:** Double-entry fraud physics ("where fake profits hide"), **8 Specialized Crook-Catching Ratios** (Implied Cash Yield $<2\%$, Cash Tax Paid / PBT $<12\%$, Capex Siphoning via Gross Block Turnover & Zombie CWIP, Depreciation Drift, Suspense Asset Ratio, Off-Balance-Sheet Guarantees, Promoter Greed Ratio $>8\%$, and Operating RPT Leakage), and the **4 Crook Founder Archetypes**.
